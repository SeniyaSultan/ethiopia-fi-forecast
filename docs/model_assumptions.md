## Validation: Telebirr Launch (May 2021)

Our impact model estimates that Telebirr's launch increased mobile money account ownership by +4 percentage points over 2 years.

- Actual change (2021→2024): from 4.7% to 9.45% (+4.75pp)
- Our model predicted: +4.0pp
